# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hannah and Fidel’s invited wedding guests, checking information before the celebration.

## Product Purpose

Help guests prepare for the wedding and find the wedding-day timeline and their reception seating.

## Operating Context

Guests reopen their original personalized invitation link to access the website. A floating button leads to the reception seating plan.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Reception seating is assigned. The first few ceremony rows are reserved for the entourage. Guests may choose any of the remaining ceremony chairs. Shared email reminders must direct guests to their original invitation links.

## Brand Commitments

Warm, personal messages signed Hannah & Fidel. Website-update emails retain the existing email’s typography and voice, with the original burgundy surround and cream letter. Preserve the authored palette in light and dark modes wherever email clients support it.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing email: `emails/wedding-update.html`. Website update reminder: `emails/last-wedding-update.html`.
