# Guest cancellations after publishing seating

Use this procedure for confirmed cancellations. Leave every remaining guest in
their current seat and leave the cancelled guests' seats empty. Empty seats do
not require rearranging the plan or reducing table capacity.

## Before changing attendance

1. Sign in to the Seating Studio at `/admin/` on a desktop or tablet.
2. Open **Invitees**, choose **Reset filters**, and search for the invitation
   party. The usual **Needs attention** filter can hide already-complete parties.
3. Confirm exactly who cancelled. Note the table and seat numbers of anyone
   still attending so you can verify them before releasing the update.

Keep the invitation party and guest records; record the cancellation through the
controls below so the reason and history are retained.

## A. The whole invitation party cancels

1. On the party's card, select **Record RSVP amendment**.
2. Set **New response** to **Declined**. The attending count becomes **0**.
3. Enter a reason, for example: “Whole party confirmed they cannot attend.”
4. Select **Save audited amendment** and wait for the save to finish.
5. Verify the party is declined and its guests no longer occupy draft seats.
   Declining the whole party automatically removes their attendance and draft
   seat assignments; there is no separate attending-names save for this case.

Continue to **Publish and release the update** below.

## B. Only individual guests within a party cancel

Example: a party of four now has three attending. Keep the party accepted,
reduce its count to three, and remove only the person who cancelled.

1. On the party's card, select **Record RSVP amendment**.
2. Keep **New response** set to **Accepted**.
3. Set **New attending count** to the number still attending.
4. Enter a reason, for example: “One guest confirmed they cannot attend; three
   guests remain.”
5. Select **Save audited amendment** and wait for the save to finish.
6. In the party's named attendee checkboxes, untick only the cancelled guests.
   Keep everyone still attending checked.
7. Enter the **Reason for changing the named attendees**.
8. Select **Save attending names**. The number of checked names must match the
   new attending count for this button to be available.
9. Verify the cancelled guests' draft seats are empty and the remaining guests
   retain their original table and seat numbers.

Changing the count alone does not identify who cancelled. Complete the named
attendee step as well. If nobody in the party is attending, use procedure A.

## Publish and release the update

Attendance changes update the working records and draft. The guest Seating
Guide continues showing its previously released revision until you publish and
release the replacement.

1. Finish the attendance changes for all confirmed cancellations.
2. Review the draft: cancelled guests have no assignments, remaining guests have
   their original seats, and the vacant seats are left empty.
3. Open **Publish**, resolve any blocking validation errors, review warnings,
   and select **Publish complete plan**. Complete the publication confirmation.
4. Open **Guest guide preview**. Under **Plan**, choose **Published revision**
   and check that it is the new revision.
5. Review the remaining names and assignments for any partially cancelled party.
   Also check an unaffected party. Complete the guide's checks for a
   single-person invitation, a party across multiple tables, and phone layout.
6. Set the optional physical-chair approval checkbox according to the actual
   chair arrangement. Only approve it if those positions are confirmed.
7. Tick the published-layout review acknowledgement, select **Release guide**,
   and confirm the displayed revision. This releases the revision for all
   invitation parties, not just the party selected in the preview.
8. Check that the guide status reports the new revision as visible to guests.
   If guest lookup is disabled, enable it in **Publish** when ready.
9. Open the existing invitation links to verify the result:
   - A partially cancelled party shows only the people still attending, with
     their original seats.
   - A fully declined party receives no seating data.
   - An unaffected party still sees its original assignments.

**Publishing alone does not update the released guest guide.** Both publication
and guide release are necessary. Existing invitation links remain valid; no
replacement links are needed. Publishing does not send automatic messages.

## Finish the event arrangements

Tell the venue or caterer the revised attendance and meal counts, subject to
your agreed deadlines. Update any separately maintained printed seating lists,
place cards, or meal lists. Keep the remaining guests' seat numbers unchanged
when coordinating physical setup.

For more detail on the release controls, see the
[Guest Seating Guide runbook](guest-seating-guide.md).
