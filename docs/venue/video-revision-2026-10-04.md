## Owner venue-recognition review before guest release

Compare Ceremony, Pavilion, Overhead and Waterfront against the video. Check:

- whether pavilion/plaza spacing feels right with fixtures hidden;
- whether planting beds, stair position, terrace edge and walls match the setting;
- whether the pavilion roof/counter and separate resort dome are recognizable;
- whether lawn depth and sand width reflect the venue rather than the schematic;
- whether any approximate feature materially misrepresents what guests encounter.

Save corrected estimates only after reviewing the proposed geometry and existing
table boundaries. The revision does not write guest records, assignments or shared
layouts automatically. Numeric dimensions remain estimates until measured. A
venue-recognition review can approve resemblance; it cannot verify dimensions.

The simplified resort façade, shoreline outline, and distant-shore context remain
schematic. This is a review candidate, not a photorealistic digital twin.
